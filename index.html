Who We Are

FinRx is a personal finance platform in development for professionals who want a clearer picture of their money. We are combining thoughtful design with AI-assisted tools to make financial planning simpler, more personalized, and easier to act on.

Built for Real-Life Finances

Whether you earn a steady salary, work independently, or manage several sources of income, your financial life deserves more than a one-size-fits-all budget. FinRx is designed to bring income, spending, taxes, and goals into one understandable view.

What We Do

FinRx is being built as an AI-powered personal finance companion for professionals. Our goal is to make it easier to organize everyday finances, anticipate future needs, and turn financial information into practical next steps.

AI-Assisted Bookkeeping

Organize income and expenses into meaningful categories so you can see where your money comes from and where it goes.

Cash Flow Forecasting

Look ahead at expected income, bills, and upcoming expenses so you can plan for both busy and quieter months.

Tax Preparation Support

Keep important records organized and make tax-related planning a more manageable part of your financial routine.

Safe-to-Spend Guidance

Get a clearer sense of what you can spend after considering planned expenses, tax reserves, and savings priorities.

Budgeting & Goals

Set priorities, follow your progress, and connect day-to-day choices to the financial goals that matter to you.

Personalized Financial Insights

Turn financial activity into understandable insights and recommendations that help you make more informed decisions.
